\subsection{Treewidth}
\label{subsec:np-treewidth}

We apply Lemma~\ref{lem:tw-replication} to the parity witness graph
from Section~\ref{subsec:np-treedepth}. For a fixed integer $R\geq1$,
take $R$ disjoint copies without changing their source edges, then complete the three global
parts to cliques, obtaining $K_R(G_F)$. This is the replicated
adaptation of Bonnet's co-tripartite completion
\cite[Section~3.1]{BonnetTreewidth}, described and proved in
Section~\ref{sec:tw-transfer}. Processing copies separately in each
decomposition path controls the additional bag size independently of $R$.

\begin{proof}[Dense treewidth gap: proof sketch]
The parity witness graph has parts of size $3m$ and
$\vc(G_F)=13m/2-\OPT(F)>3m$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:xor-cover}.
The source promise and Lemma~\ref{lem:tw-replication} therefore
give YES width at most $R(11/2+\epsilon)m+3m-1$ and NO width at
least $R(6-\epsilon)m-1$. The limiting coefficient ratio is
$12/11$: choose a small source error and then a large fixed $R$
to make the cover gap dominate the temporary bag cost $3m$.
Appendix~\ref{app:dense-np-tw} supplies explicit choices and
verifies the strict gap for every $m\geq1$.
\end{proof}
