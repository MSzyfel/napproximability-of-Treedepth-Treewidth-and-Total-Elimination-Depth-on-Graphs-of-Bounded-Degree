\subsection{Treewidth}
\label{subsec:np-treewidth}

We apply Lemma~\ref{lem:tw-replication} to the parity witness graph
from Section~\ref{subsec:np-treedepth}. Its vertex-cover number exceeds
every part size, so the transfer lemma scales the cover gap with an
additive error controlled by the replication count $R$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:tw-hardness}
For every fixed real $1\leq r<12/11$, approximating treewidth within
factor $r$ on simple unweighted graphs is NP-hard. The output graphs
have a specified partition into three equal cliques.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The leading NO and YES coefficients below approach $6$ and $11/2$
as the source error decreases and $R$ increases, giving ratio
$12/11$. Fix $1\le r<12/11$. Since $6-r(11/2)>0$, we can choose
a fixed $0<\epsilon<1/4$ such that
$6-\epsilon>r(11/2+\epsilon)$, then a fixed integer $R\ge1$ with
$R(6-\epsilon-r(11/2+\epsilon))>3r$.
The first choice preserves the separation after the source error,
and the second makes the additional YES cost $3m$ small relative
to the terms multiplied by $R$.
Apply the witness construction of Section~\ref{subsec:np-treedepth} to
the normalized source of Theorem~\ref{thm:xor-source}. The resulting
graph $G_F$ has $9m$ vertices, three independent parts of size $3m$, and
$\vc(G_F)=13m/2-\OPT(F)$.
Since $\OPT(F)\leq m$, every instance satisfies
$\vc(G_F)\geq13m/2-m=11m/2>3m$. The source promise gives the two bounds
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \textup{YES:}\quad
 \vc(G_F)&\leq\frac{13m}{2}-(1-\epsilon)m
 =\left(\frac{11}{2}+\epsilon\right)m,\\
 \textup{NO:}\quad
 \vc(G_F)&\geq\frac{13m}{2}-\left(\frac12+\epsilon\right)m
 =(6-\epsilon)m.
\end{aligned}
\]
Construct $K_R(G_F)$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:tw-replication} yields respectively
\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 \textup{YES:}&\operatorname{tw}(K_R(G_F))
       \leq R(11/2+\epsilon)m+3m-1,\\[2pt]
 \textup{NO:}&\operatorname{tw}(K_R(G_F))
       \geq R(6-\epsilon)m-1.
 \end{array}
\]
For every $m\ge1$, the NO bound minus $r$ times the YES bound is
\[
 \bigl(R(6-\epsilon)-r(R(11/2+\epsilon)+3)\bigr)m+(r-1)>0,
\]
by the choice of $R$ and $r\ge1$. Thus the bounds are separated by
factor $r$ even with the additive constants $-1$. Taking disjoint
copies of $F$ preserves the source promise and makes $m$ arbitrarily large.
The output has $9Rm$ vertices, and its three global cliques have
equal size $3Rm$. 
\end{proof}
