\subsection{Binary junction-tree total state space}
\label{app:state-space-gap}

We give the gap calculation used in
Section~\ref{subsec:total-state-space}. Fix $0<\chi<1/18$ for the
NP reduction, or $0<\chi<1/6$ for the UGC reduction.

\begin{proof}[State-space gap]
Let $G$ be the output graph, put $n=|V(G)|$, and let $R\ge1$ be the
integer number of copies. In the NP reduction, $m$ is the number of
source parity equations and $\epsilon$ its error parameter from
Section~\ref{subsec:np-treewidth}. In the UGC reduction, $N$ is the
source graph order and $\zeta$ the error parameter in
Theorem~\ref{thm:ugc-tripartite-cover} with $k=3$.
Let $w_Y$ be the YES treewidth upper bound and $w_N$ the NO treewidth
lower bound. Their differences are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:state-space-width-gaps}
\begin{aligned}
 \textup{NP:}\quad w_N-w_Y
 &\overset{\text{Lem.~\ref{lem:tw-replication}},\,\eqref{eq:np-treewidth-cover-gap}}{=}
 Rm(1/2-2\epsilon)-3m
  =\bigl(1/18-2\epsilon/9-1/(3R)\bigr)n,
   \qquad n=9Rm,\\
 \textup{UGC:}\quad w_N-w_Y
 &\overset{\text{Lem.~\ref{lem:tw-replication}, Thm.~\ref{thm:ugc-tripartite-cover}}}{=}
 RN(1/6-2\zeta)-N/3
  =\bigl(1/6-2\zeta-1/(3R)\bigr)n,
   \qquad n=RN.\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
For NP hardness, choose $0<\epsilon<1/4$ so that
$1/18-2\epsilon/9>\chi$, then take $R$ large enough that
$1/(3R)<1/18-2\epsilon/9-\chi$. For UGC, choose
$0<\zeta<1/12$ so that $1/6-2\zeta>\chi$, then take $R$ large enough that
$1/(3R)<1/6-2\zeta-\chi$. In either case, let $\delta>0$ be the
remaining margin, so
\begin{equation}\label{eq:state-space-gap-margin}
 w_N-w_Y\overset{\eqref{eq:state-space-width-gaps}}{\geq}(\chi+\delta)n.
\end{equation}
The bag bounds give
\[
 \textup{YES:}\quad\operatorname{TSS}(G)
       \overset{\eqref{eq:binary-state-width-bounds}}{\leq}n2^{w_Y+1},\qquad
 \textup{NO:}\quad\operatorname{TSS}(G)
       \overset{\eqref{eq:binary-state-width-bounds}}{\geq}2^{w_N+1}.
\]
The ratio of the NO threshold to the YES threshold satisfies
\[
 \frac{2^{w_N+1}}{n2^{w_Y+1}}
 =\frac{2^{w_N-w_Y}}n
 \overset{\eqref{eq:state-space-gap-margin}}{\geq}
       \frac{2^{(\chi+\delta)n}}n>2^{\chi n}
\]
for all sufficiently large $n$, since $2^{\delta n}>n$ then.
The source instances may be replaced by any fixed number of disjoint
copies without changing their promise fractions. Taking enough copies
therefore ensures that every output order lies in this range.
\end{proof}
