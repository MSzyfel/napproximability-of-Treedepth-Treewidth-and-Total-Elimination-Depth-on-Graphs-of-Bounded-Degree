\section*{Generative AI acknowledgement}
OpenAI Codex was used extensively in the mathematical development and
preparation of this manuscript. Its contributions included proposing
reduction gadgets and intermediate statements, developing proof attempts,
deriving approximation gaps and parameter choices, and exploring
alternative constructions. This work covered the parity, Unique Games,
and Small Set Expansion reductions, the bounded-degree and regular
extensions, and the applications. Substantial portions of the proofs and
exposition were drafted or revised with this assistance.

The author directed repeated investigations using up to ten parallel AI
agents. Agents explored stronger bounds, compared candidate constructions,
checked algebra and case analyses, and independently reviewed overlapping
parts of the arguments. They also searched for missing hypotheses,
undefined notation, unsupported uses of source results, and opportunities
to simplify proofs. AI assistance included locating and inspecting source
papers, comparing theorem statements and versions, checking citations, and
spelling out the changes to constructions inherited from the literature.

Codex generated and executed scripts for finite gadget checks, algebraic
and parameter calculations, and manuscript consistency checks. It also
edited the LaTeX sources, reorganized proofs and appendices, standardized
notation, prepared reduction diagrams, compiled the manuscript, and
inspected rendered pages. The mathematical checks consisted of model
reasoning, source inspection, and finite computational checks; they did
not constitute formal verification of the proofs.

The author supplied the research direction, selected the questions and
target bounds, reviewed and questioned arguments during development, and
decided which results and presentation changes to retain. Responsibility
for the manuscript, its mathematical claims, and its references rests
with the author.
