\section{Open questions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Can treedepth hardness be made superconstant? Our SSE result excludes
every fixed factor but leaves an $o(\log n)$ approximation possible,
while the upper bound is $O(\log n)$~\cite{Anand2026}.

Can treedepth's NP-hardness factor exceed $18/17$, or can ordinary
UGC exclude every fixed factor? For treewidth, our factors are
$12/11$ and $4/3$; SSEH excludes every fixed factor~\cite{APW}.

For total depth, the $8/7$ UGC lower bound remains far from the
$O(\sqrt{\log n})$ approximation inherited from average-case graph
search~\cite{Szyfelbein2025GraphSearch}, with the confirming-query
convention handled by~\eqref{eq:search-identification-offset}.
NP-hardness below $63/62$
holds even at bounded degree
(Corollary~\ref{cor:total-depth-bounded-degree}).

Our degree bounds depend on the approximation ratio. Can comparable
gaps be proved for graphs of a prescribed small maximum degree?
